# Research: Practical AI Use Cases

This document outlines key real-world applications of Artificial Intelligence, with a specific focus on modern Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems.

---

## 1. Enterprise Knowledge Management (RAG)
* **What it is:** Combining LLMs with external vector databases to query private documents securely without retraining the model.
* **Real-World Use:** Companies use this to let employees instantly search through thousands of internal policy PDFs, HR manuals, or technical documentation.
* **Benefit:** Eliminates LLM hallucinations by forcing the model to cite specific source materials.

## 2. Customer Support Automation
* **What it is:** AI chatbots integrated into live customer databases and product FAQs.
* **Real-World Use:** E-commerce and software platforms use RAG-driven agents to resolve customer tickets by pulling real-time tracking numbers or updated documentation.

## 3. Specialized Industry Research (Legal & Medical)
* **What it is:** High-precision search tools tailored for domain experts.
* **Real-World Use:** Legal assistants use AI to query decades of case law, while healthcare professionals use it to surface relevant medical trial data quickly.
